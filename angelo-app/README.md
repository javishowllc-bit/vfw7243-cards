## American Legion emblem source

File: legion-emblem.png, also embedded in the center of qr.png.
Source: Wikimedia Commons, File: American Legion Seal SVG.svg
Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:American_Legion_Seal_SVG.svg
Direct file: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cf/American_Legion_Seal_SVG.svg
License note on Commons: public domain, consists only of simple geometric shapes or text. The emblem may still be subject to trademark and American Legion use rules, so use is for this personal Post card only. The PNG in this folder was rendered locally from that SVG at 1024 px wide because the Commons thumbnail service rejected the requested size.

The QR was built at error correction Level H. The white padded center box is 25 percent of the QR width and the emblem itself is about 19 to 20 percent, so scanners can still read the code. Verified locally with zxingcpp and with OpenCV QRCodeDetectorAruco, both decoded the full vCard.

Note: this is American Legion Post 359, not VFW Post 7243. No USMC Eagle, Globe and Anchor is used.

Homepage in the saved contact: https://www.facebook.com/NorwalkAmericanLegionPost359Family/ (Norwalk American Legion Post 359 Family Facebook page, as listed on mylegion.org Post Detail for Post 359). It is the vCard URL field, so iPhone shows it as 'homepage'.
